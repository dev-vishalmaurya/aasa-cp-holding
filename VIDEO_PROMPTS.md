# AASA website — video prompt pack

Generate these clips in your AI video tool (Veo / Google Flow, Kling, Runway, etc.).
Each clip is one section of the home page, in page order. Together they read as one continuous
camera journey through Dubai: every clip **ends moving in the direction the next clip starts moving**.

## Settings for every clip

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect | 16:9 landscape (keep the main subject in the centre third so a 9:16 phone crop still works) |
| Resolution | Highest the tool offers; 4K (3840×2160) if it has 4K or a 4K upscale, otherwise 1080p |
| Length | 8 seconds (6–10 s is fine) |
| Frame rate | 24 fps |
| Audio | None (the site plays muted) |
| Movement | One slow, steady camera move per clip (gimbal or drone smooth) — no cuts inside a clip |

**Continuity tip:** if your tool supports *first frame / last frame* or *image-to-video*, take the
last frame of the previous clip as the first frame of the next. This makes the joins feel like one
camera turning, not a new clip.

## Style block (paste at the end of every prompt)

> Photorealistic cinematic footage, shot on a cinema camera with a 35mm lens, real-world Dubai
> architecture with correct proportions, natural physically accurate lighting, high dynamic range,
> gentle film grain, smooth stabilised camera movement, realistic reflections on glass and water,
> documentary realism, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no signage brands, no close-up faces.

## Negative prompt (if the tool has a field for it)

> cartoon, anime, illustration, CGI look, 3D render, video game, plastic textures, oversaturated
> colours, neon glow, warped or melting buildings, bent straight lines, morphing objects, extra limbs,
> distorted faces, readable text, logos, airline liveries, watermark, fisheye, shaky camera, fast cuts,
> timelapse flicker
